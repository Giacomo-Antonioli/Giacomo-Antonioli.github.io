I work on quantum numerical linear algebra: how the structure of a matrix can be used to build cheaper quantum circuits for it. My PhD at the University of Pisa (expected February 2027) is supervised by Gianna Maria Del Corso (University of Pisa) and Roel Van Beeumen (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory). Thesis: Quantum Linear Algebra: From Matrix Representations to Structured Algorithms and Computational Realization.

Research interests

  • Block encodings of structured matrices (semiseparable, hierarchical low-rank / HODLR).
  • Quantum algorithms for matrix functions f(A)b, linear systems and Krylov-type methods; tailored alternatives to QSVT.
  • Resource-aware circuits (depth, ancillas) and quantum circuit simulation (QCLAB, QCLAB++).
  • Quantum machine learning: variational circuits for state preparation and classification, quantum orthogonal neural networks.
